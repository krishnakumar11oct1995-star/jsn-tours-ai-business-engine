Day by Day Schedule

The 5-Day Sacred Circuit Itinerary

Planned with comfortable driving hours, leisurely temple darshans, and zero rushed queues.

Day 1 Madurai Arrival

Hyderabad to Madurai & Goddess Meenakshi VIP Darshan

Morning departure from Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD) on direct IndiGo flight to Madurai. Check-in at hotel, freshen up, and savor hot Telugu meals. Afternoon special VIP darshan of Sri Meenakshi Sundareswarar Temple admiring the 1,000-pillar hall. Evening scenic drive to Rameswaram island across the famous Pamban Sea Bridge. Check-in at hotel in Rameswaram.

✈️ Direct Flight (1h 40m) 🛕 Meenakshi VIP Entry 🌉 Pamban Bridge Sunset
Day 2 Rameswaram

Sacred 22 Teertha Snanam & Ramanathaswamy Jyotirlinga Darshan

Early morning holy sea bath at Agnitheertham. Enter Sri Ramanathaswamy Temple where dedicated assistants help our pilgrims complete all 22 sacred well snanams (from Mahalakshmi to Koti Teertham). Change into dry clothes and proceed for Sparsha Darshanam of the Jyotirlingam established by Lord Sri Rama. Walk through the majestic third corridor of 1,212 granite pillars. Evening visit to Ramar Padam (footprints of Lord Rama) with panoramic island views.

🌊 22 Teertha Snanam 🔱 Ramanathaswamy Darshan 🍛 Hot Telugu Satvik Meals
Day 3 Dhanushkodi

Dhanushkodi Ram Setu, APJ Abdul Kalam Memorial & Kanyakumari Drive

Post breakfast, embark on coastal journey to Dhanushkodi ghost town and Arichal Munai, where the calm waters of the Bay of Bengal meet the waves of the Indian Ocean. Witness the starting rocks of the legendary Ram Setu (Adam's Bridge). On the return, pay homage at the inspiring Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam National Memorial. Enjoy a hearty Telugu lunch before boarding our comfortable AC coach for the scenic drive to Kanyakumari at India's southern tip.

🏝️ Arichal Munai (Ram Setu) 🏛️ Kalam National Memorial 🚌 Highway Drive to Cape
Day 4 Kanyakumari

Kanyakumari Sunrise, Vivekananda Rock Memorial & Triveni Sangam Sunset

Witness the breathtaking sunrise over the Indian Ocean horizon. After breakfast, board the ferry to the iconic Vivekananda Rock Memorial and the 133-foot Thiruvalluvar Statue. Visit the ancient 3,000-year-old Sri Kanyakumari Bhagavathi Amman Temple with priority entry. Rest in the afternoon. At dusk, experience the spectacular sunset where three bodies of water (Bay of Bengal, Arabian Sea, and Indian Ocean) converge at Triveni Sangam.

🌅 Ocean Sunrise & Sunset 🚢 Vivekananda Ferry 🛕 Bhagavathi Amman Temple
Day 5 Return Home

Suchindram Temple & Return to Hyderabad with Sacred Theertham

Morning visit to the magnificent Suchindram Thanumalayan Temple, famous for musical pillars and the 22-foot monolithic Hanuman statue representing the Trinity of Shiva, Vishnu, and Brahma in a single lingam. Savor farewell Telugu satvik lunch. Transfer to Trivandrum Airport (TRV - 85 km) or Madurai Airport for direct flight back to Rajiv Gandhi International Airport, Hyderabad. Return with bottles of holy 22 Teertham and lifelong memories.

🛕 Suchindram Musical Pillars ✈️ Return to Hyderabad 💧 Holy 22 Teertham Bottles
