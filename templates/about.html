The Journey

Dedicated to Matru-Pitru Seva in Every Yatra

As a lifelong devotional traveler and creator behind the Telugu Traveller Ramesh YouTube channel, Ramesh has escorted hundreds of devotees from Hyderabad and across Telangana to ancient shrines like Kashi, Kedarnath, Badrinath, Ayodhya, and Muktinath.

Traditional commercial tour agencies often pack 50 people into a bus, give cold unfamiliar meals, and leave senior citizens walking miles through heavy temple crowds. Jai Srimannarayana Tours operates completely differently: small, intimate batches of 20–25 pilgrims, pre-booked VIP darshan passes, and personal hands-on care at every single shrine.

"For us, this is not a commercial business: it is sacred seva. When an elderly parent has peaceful tears of joy seeing Lord Shiva in Kashi or Lord Rama in Ayodhya without getting exhausted, that is our true blessing."
Our Core Promises

Why Telugu Families Choose Us

🍲

100% Telugu Satvik Bhojanam

Even in remote Himalayan valleys or Northern ghats, our accompanying South Indian kitchen team prepares hot Telugu meals: Idli, Dosa, Rice, Pappu, Sambar, Rasam, and Curd. Clean, hygienic, and comforting for elderly stomachs.

♿

Senior Citizen Wheelchair Care

Ground-floor hotel rooms guaranteed. Pre-booked battery carts and wheelchairs with attendants at all temple premises. No rushing, no standing in hours-long general lines.

🛡️

Fixed Transparent Pricing

Zero surprise costs during the tour. All prices are clearly quoted in Indian Rupees (₹) upfront, covering transfers, verified AC hotel stays, meals, and temple pass facilitation.
