Step-by-Step Spiritual Journey

Detailed 5-Day Itinerary

Planned to prevent fatigue for senior citizens, with leisurely darshan slots and timely Telugu meals.

1

Day 1: Hyderabad to Varanasi : Ganga Harathi Spectacular

Flight / Train Arrival

Devotees assemble at Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD) for direct IndiGo flight to Varanasi (or arrive via Danapur Express at Varanasi Junction). Receive traditional welcome by Ramesh’s team. Transfer by AC coach to comfortable 3-star hotel. Fresh Telugu satvik lunch and afternoon rest. In the evening, private wooden boat ride on the holy Ganges. Experience the mesmerizing Ganga Maha Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat from the water.

📍 Stay: Varanasi 3-Star AC 🍽️ Meals: Lunch, Dinner (Telugu Satvik)
2

Day 2: Kashi Vishwanath Golden Temple, Annapurna & Kalabhairava

Kashi Dham VIP Darshan

Early morning sacred holy dip in the holy Ganges River at Manikarnika / Dashashwamedh Ghat. Fast-track Sugam VIP Darshan of Lord Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga through the grand corridor. Worship at Sri Annapurna Devi Mandir and Vishalakshi Shakti Peetha. Return to hotel for South Indian breakfast. In the evening, darshan at Sri Kalabhairava (Kshetrapalaka of Kashi) and Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple.

📍 Stay: Varanasi 3-Star AC 🍽️ Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner (Telugu Satvik)
3

Day 3: Varanasi to Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam) : Drive to Ayodhya

Sangam Snanam & Rituals

Morning AC coach journey to Prayagraj (2.5 hours). Private boat to Triveni Sangam (sacred confluence of Holy Ganga, Yamuna & invisible Saraswati). Holy Sangam bath and sacred ancestral rituals (Pinda Pradanam, Veni Daanam) performed with learned Telugu purohits. Darshan at Bade Hanuman Mandir and Alopi Devi Shakti Peetha. After hot lunch, scenic afternoon drive to Ayodhya (3.5 hours). Check-in and evening Saryu River Aarti.

📍 Stay: Ayodhya AC Hotel 🍽️ Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner (Telugu Satvik)
4

Day 4: Ayodhya Ram Janmabhoomi, Hanumangarhi & Kanak Bhavan

Ram Lalla Darshan

Grand morning VIP Darshan of Lord Ram Lalla at the newly consecrated Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir. Experience the divine sanctum sanctorum and circumambulate the temple complex. Worship at Hanumangarhi Temple (the guardian of Ayodhya) and Kanak Bhavan (palace of Sita and Rama). Visit Dasaratha Mahal and Surya Kund. Evening transfer towards airport / railway station.

📍 Stay: AC Hotel / Overnight Return 🍽️ Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
5

Day 5: Arrival in Hyderabad

Home Sweet Home

Arrive back in Hyderabad (Rajiv Gandhi International Airport / Secunderabad Station) carrying sacred Kashi Ganga Theertham, Ayodhya Akshatas, and lifelong spiritual memories with your family.

📍 Drop: Hyderabad (RGIA / Secunderabad) 🙏 Jai Srimannarayana!
