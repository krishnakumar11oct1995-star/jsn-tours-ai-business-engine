Day-by-Day Festival Schedule

Complete 5-Day Festival Itinerary

Crafted for senior citizens and families with balanced walking distances and dedicated seating.

Day 1: Hyderabad to Varanasi : Evening Ganga Ghat Diya Illumination

Arrival & Sunset Boat

Devotees assemble at Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD) for direct IndiGo flight to Varanasi (or arrive via Secunderabad Danapur Express). Escorted transfer by private AC coach to hotel. Fresh South Indian Telugu satvik lunch. In the evening, private chartered boat ride along the Ganges. Witness thousands of floating earthen diyas and the world-renowned Dashashwamedh Ghat Ganga Maha Aarti.

📍 Hotel Stay: Varanasi (AC Rooms) 🍽️ Meals: Lunch, Dinner (Fresh Telugu Satvik)

Day 2: Kashi Vishwanath Dham VIP Darshan, Annapurna & Kalabhairava

Kashi Jyotirlinga Darshan

Morning holy dip in the sacred Ganges at Manikarnika Ghat. Sugam (VIP) Darshan at Kashi Vishwanath Golden Temple with zero waiting in general crowds. Worship at Sri Annapurna Devi Mandir and Sri Vishalakshi Shakti Peetha. Afternoon Telugu meal and rest. Evening darshan at Sri Kalabhairava Temple (the guardian of Kashi) and Sankat Mochan Hanuman Mandir.

📍 Hotel Stay: Varanasi 🍽️ Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 3: Varanasi to Prayagraj Sangam : Drive to Holy Ayodhya

Triveni Sangam Snanam

Morning AC coach journey to Prayagraj (2.5 hours). Private boat to Triveni Sangam (confluence of Holy Ganga, Yamuna & subterranean Saraswati). Sacred Snanam, Deepadanam, and ancestral rituals conducted by Telugu-speaking purohits. Darshan at Bade Hanuman (reclining posture) and Alopi Devi. After hot lunch, afternoon drive to Ayodhya (3.5 hours). Check-in and evening Saryu River Aarti.

📍 Hotel Stay: Ayodhya 🍽️ Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 4: Grand Ayodhya Ram Janmabhoomi Darshan & Saryu Deepotsav Celebration

Deepotsav Highlights

Morning VIP Darshan of Lord Ram Lalla at the consecrated Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir. Worship at Hanumangarhi Temple and Kanak Bhavan (palace of Sita-Rama). In the evening, witness the breathtaking spectacle of millions of earthen diyas illuminating Ram Ki Paidi and the entire riverbank of Saryu. Participate in Deepa Daanam and devotional bhajans.

📍 Hotel Stay: Ayodhya 🍽️ Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 5: Return Flight to Hyderabad

Hyderabad Arrival

Morning prayer and breakfast. Transfer to Ayodhya Airport / Lucknow Airport for direct return flight to Rajiv Gandhi International Airport, Hyderabad. Return with blessed Kashi Ganga Theertham and Ayodhya Deepotsav Akshatas for your household pooja room.

📍 Drop: Hyderabad (RGIA / Secunderabad) 🙏 జై శ్రీమన్నారాయణ!
